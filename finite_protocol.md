# Finite archive study

The primary endpoint is prediction interval half-length at 95% model-conditional coverage, in marginal target standard deviations. A reusable archive collects one unit linear score and one target per calibration object. Compare four prespecified allocations with the same objects and queries: largest marginal decrease in expected squared modulus, proportional to deployment gain with largest-remainder rounding, cycling over all coordinates in a seeded random order, and cycling over the four leading coordinates. For the spectral scenes additionally cycle over the 24 physical band groups. These designs share a ridge center and the folded-normal radius using a norm bound on the residual mean. Frozen spectral directions and allocation counts use no paired labels. The query-specific design has a different archive for each new query and is a theoretical comparator.

1. Evaluate the certified one-tail approximation and equal-tail union on scalar isotropic gains 0.5 and 1 and measurement magnitudes 0, 0.75, 1.5, 2.25, 4 and 8. Retain the prior 128-query audit.
2. Verify the dimension law at p=8,32,128 and budgets p, 4p and p squared. Use 512 independent score-energy/query draws and compare balanced coordinate archives with query-specific scores. Report the root mean squared modulus and mean honest half-width. Use Gaussian deployment with unit gains. 
3. Use all eight measured camera kernels at the original smooth, middle-noise setting. Use budgets 32 and 256, 16 paired archive replicates and 64 common held-out queries per replicate. Archive and deployment targets are generated at a fixed norm-0.8 coupling equally mixing the leading coordinate and a unit vector spread evenly over all remaining coordinates. Report half-width quantiles, realized coverage, squared modulus and conditional-mean estimation error. Each row aggregates one archive and its queries.
4. Evaluate on the corrected Indian Pines and Salinas AVIRIS cubes. Use every pixel. Divide the first 144 retained bands into 24 consecutive groups of six and use their means as the visible variables. Predict the average of the final 16 retained bands. Partition each image into four contiguous row strips using floor(4*row/height). In rotation j use strip j for visible marginal moments, j+1 for target-only moments, j+2 for paired calibration, and j+3 for testing (modulo four). Fit the eigensystem solely on the visible strip. Add simulated deployment noise of standard deviation 0.2 times the root mean input variance per visible group, in original group units. Use every fourth test row and column, selecting the first row of that strip and column zero as offsets. Use 16 independent random archives, sampled without replacement within each replicate, at each of the same two budgets. The same test-noise realization is shared across methods within each replicate. Compare empirical coverage, median and 90th-percentile half-width and RMSE. These descriptive results include marginal estimation error, non-Gaussianity and spatial shift. No distribution-free coverage claim is intended.
5. Use seed 20261124 for the revision. Retain all cells irrespective of sign. Compare means of per-archive summaries within each kernel/scene; report individual fold ranges for the scenes. Re-run in the same project environment and compare every output row.
