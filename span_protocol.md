# Comparison conditional on knowing the batch span

This supplemental comparison was specified after the Gaussian batch results of the main study. It distinguishes access to a batch's subspace from knowing how strongly different queries use directions inside the subspace. Hold p=64, batch size 16, budget 256, span and all query norms fixed. Use a Hadamard query matrix with equal spectral energy or one direction carrying 75% of total energy and the other 15 sharing the remainder. Compare exact integer allocation, the mean-information allocation, equal allocation inside the known span, and equal allocation over all 64 coordinates. Compute expected squared modulus by integrating the known chi-square law. Use 256 freshly seeded matched archives per regime for RMS half-width and coverage. The target and Gaussian assumptions are held fixed across designs. Fit no parameters to the outcomes.
